LLEO LAB

A shared way of working.

Start small. Make your work understandable. Give people credit.

  1. Create your own GitHub account, enable two-factor authentication, and share your username with the project lead.
  2. Read the public/private policy before uploading anything.
  3. Practice a small edit on a branch and open a pull request.
  4. Agree on your project question, responsibilities, and storage with the lead.
  5. Choose whether and how you want to appear on the People page.

Before the first commit

This repository and its history are public. Do not upload participant records, credentials, consent forms, identifying recordings, or private lab notes. A private GitHub repository is not a substitute for approved research-data storage.
