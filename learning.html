How does what we know shape what we can learn?

Prior experience can support new learning, but it can also make useful information harder to notice. We connect cue learning, prediction error, adaptation, and lexical experience.

How we investigate

Compare learning histories, track changing cue use, and test how knowledge transfers to a new vocabulary.
